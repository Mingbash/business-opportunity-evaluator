# Worked example — fictional local service

All inputs below are synthetic. This is an illustrative output, not live market research or a customer claim.

## Input

Evaluate weekend bicycle repair pickup for residents in one town as a side hustle. One person can work six hours/week and already owns repair tools. Offer: collect, repair simple punctures/brake adjustments, and return within an agreed window. Proposed price is 45 currency units per job. No confirmed buyers or price acceptance. Assume parts 8, transport 6 and payment costs 2 per job. Delivery plus coordination takes 1.5 hours. No current web access.

## Illustrative output

**Scope:** supplied town; side hustle; current market conditions unverified. **Atomic business:** residents with a repair need buy a bounded pickup/repair/return service per job, acquired through a still-unvalidated local route. **Solution:** remove a trip to a repair shop for residents who value pickup enough to pay for it.

| Unit economics | U | Scenario is positive, but actual price acceptance/costs are unverified. |

| Business-model quality | 2 | Under supplied scenario, labor grows with each job; little operating leverage. |
| Timing/durability | U | Seasonality and current supply unknown. |

**Scenario, not forecast:** 45 − 8 − 6 − 2 = 29 contribution before additional costs/tax and founder labor. At 1.5 hours/job, nominal contribution is about 19.3/hour before acquisition, rework and idle time. Six hours supports at most four jobs only if those omitted activities fit elsewhere; it is not a sales forecast.

**Market Structure:** undetermined/low confidence. **Current Entry:** provisional C/low confidence. **Overall:** provisional C/low confidence: research and cheap validation, no major commitment. Missing evidence is not a zero score. The limited labor model does not itself make a side hustle invalid.

**Next decision:** obtain local competitor/service-price evidence and test whether residents accept the scoped price and pickup window. Do not spend on equipment before evidence changes that decision. Stop or redesign if collected revenue fails to cover actual complete costs or if safe, permitted repair/transport cannot be established. The original local direct service remains separate from any shop partnership repair variant.
