## Frame of reference / 参考系

The method evolved while comparing AI-assisted services, digital products, local-service delivery and different acquisition routes. The original research briefs called for marketplace evidence, competing products/services, official access/payment rules, practitioner claims and counterexamples. Those were inputs to test—not proof that a category worked for every entrant. This skill is not attributed to a single named business framework or guru; no evidence supports that claim.

方法形成于对 AI 辅助服务、数字产品、本地服务及不同获客方式的反复比较。原研究任务要求参考市场平台证据、竞品与替代服务、准入和收款规则、从业者主张及反例。它们是待检验的输入，不是某类业务普遍成立的证明。没有证据支持把本 skill 归为某一位导师或某一个现成商业框架的衍生品，因此不作这种署名。

The durable distinction is between market structure, entry feasibility and the current decision. Separate main-business and side-hustle objectives; include acquisition, trust-building, collection and ongoing work; preserve the original offer before proposing repairs. Historical personal revenue targets are not shipped as default success thresholds. The public version also removes mandatory global sizing and spreadsheet output, while retaining the evidence/unknown boundary.

保留下来的主线是分别判断市场结构、进入可行性和当前行动；区分主业与副业目标；计入获客、信任建立、收款和持续劳动；提出改进前先评原方案。过去个人收入目标不成为默认达标线。公开版本还移除了强制全球估算和工作簿输出，但保留证据与未知的边界。
